# Margalla Institute of Technology: Fee Schedule (Fall 2027)

## 1. Tuition

| Programme | Fee per credit hour (PKR) |
| --- | --- |
| BS (all disciplines) | 11,000 |
| MS | 13,800 |

## 2. Other charges

| Charge | Amount (PKR) |
| --- | --- |
| Late registration | 3,300 |
| Transcript (per copy) | 1,700 |
| Campus transfer | 5,500 |

## 3. Refunds

3.1 Tuition is refunded in full when a course is dropped within the first 10 days of classes, and at 50 percent between day 11 and day 20. No refund is given after day 20.
