# Examination Re-evaluation Procedure

## 1. Application

1.1 A student may apply for re-evaluation of a final paper within seven days of the result.

1.2 The fee is 2,000 rupees per course, refunded if the grade changes.

## 3. Outcome

3.1 The re-evaluated grade replaces the original even when it is lower.
