# Library Borrowing Rules

## 1. Loan period

1.1 A student may borrow up to five books at a time for fourteen days.

1.2 A loan may be renewed once if nobody has reserved the book.

## 2. Fines

2.1 A late book costs 20 rupees per day. A student with unpaid fines cannot borrow new books.
